In this figure

A basic LCD controller gives no data. The INA226 puts a tiny shunt resistor in the battery + wire and measures the voltage across it: current = voltage ÷ resistance. The ESP32 reads it over I2C and sends it over WiFi. The "App preview" below can later become the real app. Open the 🔧 multimeter (M) and measure the few millivolts across the shunt yourself.
