In this figure

Inside, a 12V battery is 6 cells of ~2.1V each. Lead plates and acid (soaked into glass mat) store chemical energy. Voltage only tells you the charge after ~1 hour of rest. The deeper you drain it, the faster it ages. Open the 🔧 meter and probe the terminals, or the cells in the cutaway.
