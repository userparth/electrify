In this figure

The inverter turns the battery's 12V DC into 230V AC. It connects straight to the battery with thick wire and a 30–40A fuse, not to the controller LOAD. Every AC watt pulls more than ~1/12 A from the battery, and an idle inverter still eats 4–7W. Open the 🔧 multimeter to probe the battery, fuse, inverter input and the AC socket.
