How to use

  1. Move the meter: drag its yellow body anywhere.
  2. Probes: drag a probe tip; it snaps to the nearest dot (a label shows what it is). Drop it in empty space to leave it hanging.
  3. Knob: click a mode label (OFF, V, Ω, ·)), A), click the knob to step, or scroll on it.
  4. FUNC toggles AC/DC in V and A. The meter starts in AC on purpose, like the real one.
  5. Sockets: click 10A or VΩmA to move the red plug. Black always stays in COM.
  6. HOLD freezes the display. After a mistake, click ✕ replace fuse on the meter.
