Step 1 of the plan: a second 55W panel of the same model, wired in parallel with an MC4 Y-branch. In parallel the currents add (~5.2A) and the voltage stays the same. In series the voltages add (~49V Voc), which is at or above the controller's limit. Over time the battery becomes the bottleneck too (step 3). Open the 🔧 multimeter and probe any dot.
