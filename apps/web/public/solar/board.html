How to use

  1. Add a part: drag it from the left onto the board (or click it).
  2. Move: drag the part's body.
  3. Wire: press on a terminal dot and drag to another terminal dot.
  4. Remove: click a part or wire, then Del / Backspace.
  5. Measure: M opens the multimeter; drag its probes onto any dot. With the red plug in 10A the meter really is in the circuit.
  6. Run: time, weather and speed are below the board. Your layout is saved in this browser.

Things to try

  • Build the full setup yourself, in the safe order: battery → fuse → controller, then panel, then loads.
  • Connect the panel before the battery and see the controller pick 24V.
  • Short battery + to − with a wire, with and without a fuse in the path.
  • Feed the router straight from the battery, then through the DC-DC. Turn the trim pot to 16V.
  • Put the INA226 shunt in the battery + line and watch its current at noon and at night.
  • Swap a router's barrel wires. Run the inverter at 350W.
