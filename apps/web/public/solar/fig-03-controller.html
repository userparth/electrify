In this figure

The controller does two jobs: it feeds panel current into the battery safely (PWM switching, 3 stages), and it cuts the load before the battery runs flat (LVD). Inside are two MOSFET switches: Q1 for the panel, Q2 for the load. Open the 🔧 meter and probe any terminal.
