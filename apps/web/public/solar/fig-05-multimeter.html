A meter only tells the truth when three things are right: the knob, the socket and the probe polarity. Click the knob or sockets directly, change the target, and watch the display. The biggest danger: red probe in the 10A socket, then touching the battery. The 🔧 draggable meter works on these same targets too.
  1. Black always in COM
  2. Voltage: red in VΩmA, knob V, FUNC to DC
  3. Current: red in 10A, knob A, panel only
  4. Afterwards, red straight back to VΩmA
