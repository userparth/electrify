A wire is a resistor too. Thin, long or aluminium wire drops voltage and makes heat. A bad joint (lug, ferrule, MC4) becomes the hottest spot. A fuse protects the wire, but only when it sits near the source and is rated below the wire. Open the 🔧 multimeter and measure the drop yourself: red on source +, black on load +.
